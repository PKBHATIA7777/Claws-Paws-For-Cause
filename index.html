Who We Are

More Than Just a Rescue.

Claws & Paws For Cause started with a single bowl of milk in Noida. Today, we are a movement.

We believe that every street animal deserves dignity. Our team works round the clock to provide medical aid, sterilization, and warm meals to those who have no one else.
